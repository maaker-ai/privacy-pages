Frequently asked questions

How do I change my birthday or life expectancy?
Open the Settings tab. Your life grid and death clock update immediately.

How do I add the widgets?
Home Screen: touch and hold an empty area, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Memento. Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize → Lock Screen, then tap the widget area and choose Memento. Open the app once after setting your birthday so the widgets can show your countdown.

Where is my data stored? Is there an account?
There is no account. Everything stays on your iPhone; see the privacy policy.

I bought Memento on a new iPhone—do I pay again?
No. Download it again from the App Store with the same Apple ID.
